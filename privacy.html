Arcade Dynasty Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Arcade Dynasty is a single-player game made by Steven Luong. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: we don't collect any.

Information we collect

None. Arcade Dynasty does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. The game works fully offline.

Your saved game

Your arcade, progress and settings are saved only on your device. If your device's backups (such as iCloud Backup) are turned on, the save is included in those backups like any other app data, under your Apple account; we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes the save from your device.

Names you type in the game

Any names or sign text you enter stay on your device. They are never uploaded or shown to other players.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that uses data (for example optional cloud saves), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
